# Novartis demo script — 2026-05-11 with Thiru Pattipaka

A sequenced 8-minute walkthrough that lands the four named asks from the brief: ASCO/ESMO impact on Kisqali, Pluvicto vs RLT pipeline, cross-tumour KOLs, and the Veeva Medical_Insight loop.

Use the suggested prompts list to drive Auri — they're tuned so the canned response trail is rich even if live API mode is off.

## 0. Land on the platform (~30 sec)

**You'll see:** Login → Command Center.

**Say:** *"Aurivian is the Medical Affairs intelligence layer for Novartis Oncology. Top-level you have your Command Center; on the left the four named agents and four workspaces; on the right Auri, the chat assistant, available on every screen. Everything here is grounded in your data — Veeva Medical_Insight, ASCO/ESMO ingestion, ad boards, capture app — and every claim cites back to the source."*

**Don't:** spend more than 30 seconds on the framing.

## 3. NOVA — the field-to-strategy loop (~120 sec)

**This is the longest section because it lands the Veeva loop.**

**Click NOVA in the left rail.** Default tab: Strategy-to-Action.

**Walk through the strategic cascade briefly:** ISP → 5 Medical Objectives → 6 Listening Priorities → Insights → Actions. Point at the coverage scoring — **MO5 is at Gap.**

**Open Insight AI4 (PSMA-RLT site-of-care capacity).** Show the source rows: 3 of them are tagged `Veeva Medical_Insight`. Click into the sources and read one quote out loud. Then point at the linked actions (A7 Theranostic Centre Activation Program — Accepted, A8 site-readiness checklist — Started).

**Click "Add to Insight Journey"** on AI4 (or any Prioritised insight). Then click **Insight Journey** in the left rail. The pinned insight shows up in the Captured lane with a "Just added from NOVA" highlight.

**Switch to NOVA's KIT Performance tab.** Show the KIT scorecards (relevance, sentiment shift, AI summary current vs prior month). Highlight ASC4FIRST — sentiment shift +0.26 over the cycle.

**Switch to NOVA's Insight Sources & Impact tab.** **Veeva CRM Medical_Insight is the top row of the value matrix** — 1,843 records, ROI 8.4/10. Show the Insight-to-Impact lineage cards underneath: insight → action → measurable outcome (e.g., wait list 9.2 → 5.1 weeks).

**Say:** *"This is the loop. Field MSLs capture into Veeva. Auri reads it, recognises recurring themes, scores them against your strategic framework, and proposes actions. Medical leadership reviews and approves. The action's outcome flows back to confirm, challenge or change the strategy. Time-to-insight goes from a quarterly report to a daily feed."*
